About The Art Factory

The Art Factory Productions began as a digital content movement in 2015, evolving from viral online sketches into a full creative production collective producing films, web series, parody content and branded media.

Built independently by creators experimenting with storytelling, the studio has generated millions of organic views while continuing to explore new forms of cinematic expression.
