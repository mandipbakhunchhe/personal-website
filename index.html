The Art Factory is an independent film and media production company focused on storytelling, satire, digital entertainment and visual storytelling across film, web and branded media.

About The Art Factory

Founded from the early digital content movement in 2015, The Art Factory evolved from viral online sketches into a full creative production collective producing short films, parody content, web series, music videos and branded media.

Built independently by creators experimenting with storytelling, the company has generated millions of organic views across social media platforms while continuously exploring new formats of cinematic and digital expression.

What We Do

Film Production

Short films, sketches, satire and independent cinematic storytelling from concept to final production.

Digital Content

Online series, social media content and entertainment formats designed for modern digital audiences.

Commercial & Brand Work

Music videos, advertisements and branded storytelling collaborations with artists and companies.

Selected Work

Our Journey

Beginning as short-form comedy videos in Nepal, the collective grew rapidly through viral content and collaborative filmmaking. Over time, the team expanded into web series, satire films and commercial productions while navigating the challenges of independent filmmaking.

Despite financial setbacks, platform changes and industry limitations, The Art Factory continues to exist as a creative identity driven by one belief: art comes first.
